Save video at the resolution it was made in.

4K Downloader is a free Windows app for saving videos and playlists in up to 8K. It merges the audio and video for you and keeps a queue running in the background.

  • 1080p

4K and 8K support

Grab a video at the best quality it offers, up to 8K, or cap it at 4K, 1440p, 1080p or 720p. Video and audio arrive as separate streams, and FFmpeg merges them into a clean MP4 or MKV.

Multi-site and playlists

Powered by yt-dlp, so it works with YouTube and many other sites. Paste one link, a batch of links, or a whole playlist, then tick only the videos you want.

Built-in queue and SponsorBlock

Line up as many videos as you like and download one to three at a time. Pause, resume or cancel any item. SponsorBlock can cut sponsor, intro and outro segments out of YouTube videos when you turn it on.

Also included: a clipboard monitor that offers links as you copy them, browser cookies for age-restricted videos, embedded subtitles and cover art, and settings that are remembered between sessions.

How to use it

  1. Download and unzip

    Click Download for Windows, then extract the ZIP file to any folder you like.

  2. Choose quality and folder

    Pick a resolution, MP4 or MKV, and where finished files should go. The app remembers your choices.

  3. Paste a link

    Paste a video or playlist link and click Add to Queue. Downloads start on their own.
