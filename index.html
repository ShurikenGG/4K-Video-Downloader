Set up in three steps

  1. Download

    Windows: click Download for Windows to get the .zip.

    Android: tap Download for Android to get the .apk.

  2. Extract or install

    Windows: right-click the .zip, choose Extract All, then open Prismo from the new folder.

    Android: open the .apk and tap Allow from this source when Android asks, then tap Install.

  3. Paste or share a link

    Windows: paste a link, choose your quality, and click Add to Queue.

    Android: share a link to Prismo from your browser or another app, or paste it in.

Windows SmartScreen warning

The first time you run Prismo, Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. SmartScreen warns about apps it doesn’t recognize yet, and new independent apps often trigger it. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only run files you downloaded from the links on this page.

Android unknown-apps prompt and updates

Android asks before installing apps from outside Google Play. Choose Allow from this source for your browser or file manager, install Prismo, then switch it back off if you like. For update notifications, add the repository to Obtainium:

Questions people ask

Is Prismo really free?

Yes. Prismo is free and open source, with no ads and no file limits. The source code is in the GitHub repositories linked at the bottom of this page.

Do my links or files go through a server?

No. Prismo runs on your own device and downloads straight from the site to you. There are no middleman servers and no tracking.

Which sites does it work with?

Any site yt-dlp supports: YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Reddit, Instagram, X, Facebook and 1,000+ more. Sites change often. If one stops working on Windows, open Settings and choose Check for yt-dlp Updates to refresh the download engine.

Can it download age-restricted or sign-in-only videos?

On Windows, choose your browser under Settings, then Cookies from Browser, so Prismo can use your signed-in session. Close the browser first; Firefox is often the most reliable. Only use this for content you’re allowed to access.

Why do Windows and Android show a warning?

Prismo is a new independent app that you download outside the Microsoft Store and Google Play, so Windows SmartScreen and Android’s unknown-apps prompt may appear. Follow the steps above, and download Prismo only from the links on this page.

Is it legal to download videos?

Only download content you own, content that is in the public domain or licensed for it, or content you have permission to save. Respect each site’s terms and your local copyright law.
